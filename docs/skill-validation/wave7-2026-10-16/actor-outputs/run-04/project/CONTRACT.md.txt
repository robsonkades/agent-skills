# Accepted contract: optional post-commit receipt

The booking and stock reservation form the mandatory atomic business unit. The receipt is a best-effort local projection: a normal valid receipt must persist after the command succeeds, while an invalid receipt must not fail the already successful business call or undo its booking/reservation. Receipt body storage remains limited to 12 characters; do not truncate or increase this constraint to hide rejection. Receipt work must run only after source commit, when an independent READ_COMMITTED connection can see the booking. Receipt loss on failure or application interruption is accepted and no recovery store is required. Keep the current synchronous scheduling; no executor is requested.

Unavailable stock must fail the business command and leave no booking/receipt/reservation. A nontransactional publication must not create a receipt. Retain public BookingService.place(id,sku,receiptText), event identity, stock rule and ReceiptObserver.beforeWrite's meaning: called inside the actual writer immediately before the write. The observer is diagnostic and does not authorize changing transaction behavior. No broker, durable registry or dependency addition is authorized.

When called within an existing business transaction, the service joins that unit; its caller remains completion owner. Preserve this REQUIRED participation. A later owner failure must roll back the mandatory state; no receipt may survive a rollback of its booking.

The receipt consists of a header in receipt and body in receipt_detail. These two writes form one receipt unit: no partial header may remain if the body write fails. The 12-character limit applies to receipt_detail.body. Preserve this schema and atomic receipt behavior.
