This is a local design fixture, not runnable Envoy configuration. The installed TCP adapter uses proxy-policy.json. connect_start=first_client_data opens no upstream connection until the local client sends bytes; connect_start=immediate opens the trusted fixed route as soon as the downstream TCP connection is accepted. The route is known without payload parsing. Inspection wait and buffering are bounded by the shown fields. There are no retries or TLS termination changes in this proposal. No proxy image/version or real deployment validation is supplied.
