# Accepted client contract

Owner: measurement API maintainer. Status: accepted. All calls in this fixture are sequential and the cache belongs to this module instance. Quantities must remain exact decimal values with their units; do not convert them through binary floating point. Null amount/unit is invalid. Different units identify different measurements. Cache identity and reports must follow the measurement meaning below.

The submitted decimal scale records measurement precision and is part of its identity. Equal magnitudes reported at different precision must remain distinct measurements, and reports preserve that submitted scale.
