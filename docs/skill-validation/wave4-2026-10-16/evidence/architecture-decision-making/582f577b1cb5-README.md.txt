# Architecture decisions

Use numbered Markdown records in this directory. Preserve accepted context, decision and rationale. Status and relationship metadata may be updated. Accepted decisions name the target; current guidance remains in force until the replacement activation condition is verified. Use a scoped `Planned replacement` link while a gate is pending. Never reuse an ID.

Decision rights are in [the effective delegation table](../governance/decision-rights.csv). The named decision-maker for the scope may accept the outcome; consulted roles give advice. A signed meeting note is the evidence of an outcome when its signer has that right.
