# Account concurrency test review

The original conservation/accounting assertions accept an implementation that always refuses withdrawals. The local contract requires acceptance when the gate is free and funds are sufficient. `test/AccountTest.java` now checks uncontended withdrawals of 10 and then the remaining 90 from an initial 100, asserting both successful outcomes and exact balances.

The existing 20 stress repetitions remain: 32 virtual workers, 1,000 attempts each, a five-second start barrier, retained Futures sharing a 20-second result budget, cancellation/shutdown in `finally`, and a separate two-second termination assertion that preserves worker failures. Nonnegative balance, conservation, and complete attempt accounting are unchanged. Contention permits refusals, so neither complete depletion nor a fixed concurrent success count is asserted. The gate-holding hook was unnecessary for the free-gate positive control; a mandatory busy refusal would exceed the contract.
