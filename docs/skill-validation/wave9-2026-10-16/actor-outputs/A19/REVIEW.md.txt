RetryPlan.java now implements the scheduling policy in contract.md while preserving the public method and Plan record. The prior implementation admitted a call when completed equaled maxAttempts, used the wrong retry exponent, allowed overflowing or masked shifts, chose the smaller of jitter and server advice, and allocated attempt time without subtracting wait and reserve. Its summed admission expression could also overflow. The new verification failed against that implementation: an initial call expected an 80 ns allocation but received 90 ns because reserve was omitted.

Elapsed time is computed as now - start. Under the supplied true-elapsed interval [0, 2^63), Java's modular subtraction handles negative readings and signed clock wrap. Expiry is checked before calculating total - elapsed. Reserve and minimum work are checked before subtraction, avoiding an overflow-prone sum of wait + minimumWork + reserve. No extra duration cap or narrower accepted input range was introduced.

The first call ignores serverMinimum and does not draw randomness. For retries, exponent = completed - 1. The jitter bound equals min(delayCap, base * 2^exponent): exponent >= 63 saturates directly, while smaller exponents compare base against delayCap >> exponent before shifting. This prevents signed overflow and Java's shift-count masking, including completed values near Integer.MAX_VALUE. The supplied draw is used once when needed; wait is exactly max(draw, serverMinimum), without shortening either to fit. A wait that exceeds available - minimumWork rejects. Otherwise attemptNanos is exactly min(attemptCap, available - wait), where available already excludes reserve. Exact fits are admitted. Every rejected result has zero durations.
