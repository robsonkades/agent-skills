# RetryPlan repair

The repair preserves the public `next` signature and `Plan` record and implements the exact scheduling policy in `contract.md`. All stated valid inputs, including `Long.MAX_VALUE` durations and large attempt counts, remain supported.

The original implementation used the wrong retry exponent, allowed another call when the maximum was already reached, selected the smaller of jitter and server advice, and allocated attempt time without removing wait and reserve. Adding wait, minimum work and reserve could overflow and admit an impossible plan. The original clock expression was not independently a demonstrated defect: under this contract, modular subtraction already represents total minus elapsed. The replacement makes its elapsed-time reasoning explicit.

## Arithmetic

Calculate elapsed as `now - start`. The contract's elapsed interval below 2^63 makes this difference nonnegative even across signed wrap; absolute readings need not be positive. Reject elapsed at or beyond total, then calculate remaining as `total - elapsed`.

Compare reserve against remaining before subtracting. After subtracting reserve, require at least minimumWork. This keeps every subsequent duration subtraction nonnegative without narrowing the accepted range. Reject when `completed >= maxAttempts`; that count includes the initial call.

The initial call neither draws nor waits and ignores serverMinimum. For retries, the exponent is `completed - 1`. An exponent of 63 or more, or a base too large to shift safely, saturates the exponential at `Long.MAX_VALUE`; the delay cap then determines the positive half-open draw bound. This yields exactly `min(delayCap, base * 2^(completed-1))` in mathematical arithmetic.

Set wait to exactly `max(draw, serverMinimum)`. Reject an unfit server minimum without truncating it; after drawing, reject a wait greater than `remaining - reserve - minimumWork`. Allocate exactly `min(attemptCap, remaining - reserve - wait)`. Equality admits an exact fit. Every rejected plan contains two zero durations.

## Boundary and limits

This is a pure planning function. Its caller must recheck the remaining budget after the actual wait and intervening work, enforce attempt timeouts, and own cancellation and resource release. A plan does not bound wall-clock completion or prove remote work stopped.

Admission also does not establish retry safety: failure classification, stable request replay, ambiguous outcomes, durable idempotency/reconciliation, hidden retry layers and aggregate load budgets remain caller/integration responsibilities. No provider, client, database or framework contract exists in this exercise. No production, network, cancellation, distribution or fleet guarantee was tested. External skill routes and web references were not opened; the supplied local context is the evidence boundary.
