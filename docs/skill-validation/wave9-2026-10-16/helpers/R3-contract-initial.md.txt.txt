# Retry scheduling contract

RetryPlan.next is a pure planning function, not a sleeper or cancellation mechanism. Preserve the public method and Plan record. start and now are readings from one monotonic nanoTime source with true elapsed duration in [0,2^63); readings may be negative and may cross signed wrap. total is positive and fits long; reserve>=0; minimumWork, attemptCap, base, delayCap are positive and fit long; minimumWork<=attemptCap. completed>=0 counts calls already completed and maxAttempts>=1 includes the first call. serverMinimum>=0 is validated advice in nanoseconds. All these inputs are contract-valid, including large values. Do not add narrower limits.

Stop if completed has reached maxAttempts or remaining budget cannot fit the requested work. A rejected Plan has both durations zero. On the initial call (completed=0), do not sleep or draw randomness; serverMinimum applies only between attempts. On a retry use full jitter: the supplied uniform half-open draw [0,bound) spans min(delayCap, base*2^(completed-1)), with mathematical saturation instead of signed overflow. The supplied draw always respects its positive bound. It is not necessary to draw when already known unable to admit. The wait must be at least the larger of this local draw and the server minimum. Do not truncate server advice. Keep reserve after wait, and allocate at most attemptCap from what remains, provided minimumWork fits. Exact fit is admitted. No wall-clock conversion, sleeping, real randomness, server calls or dependency changes belong here. Callers must recheck admission after actual wait/elapsed work; a plan does not promise a wall-clock return bound.
